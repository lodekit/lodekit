You don't need to understand Lodekit's insides to use it. But a rough map helps
you know where your apps live, where your data goes, and what your agent is
actually doing. Here's the whole picture.

<BigPicture />

## One process, one folder

Two facts carry most of the mental model.

**One process.** The engine is a single program on your Mac. It hosts every
app you build, plus the dashboard, all served in your browser at
`http://lodekit.localhost:7777`. The manager — the menubar icon — starts the
engine, watches over it, and restarts it if you ask.

**One folder.** Everything you'd ever want to keep lives in the Lodekit root,
`~/Documents/Lodekit`. Inside it sit three trees, side by side: `apps/` holds
each app's source files, `data/` holds what each app stores, and `gateways/`
describes the outside providers your apps can reach. That one folder is the
thing you back up.

## Two doors

The engine has two doors, one for each of you.

**The dashboard is yours.** It shows every app with its live status, and it's
where you tune settings, browse logs, and manage secrets — all in your browser.

**Your agent has its own door.** Through a separate connection, the agent can
create apps, open them for you, and read an app's logs and data on your
behalf — so it can answer "show me all my recipes" or debug an app it built.
You'll set that connection up in
[Connect your agent](/start/connect-your-agent/).

## How an app gets built

When your agent creates or changes an app, all it does is write files into
`apps/`. Lodekit's watcher notices the change, builds the app with its own
build tools, and serves the result:

<BuildLifecycle />

You never run a build. Save (or let your agent save), and the app's card on the
dashboard flips to *building…*, then *ready*. If something goes wrong, the card
shows the error instead — and your agent can read the details and fix it.

## Where your data lives

Each app keeps what it stores under `data/` in its own space, right next to the
`apps/` tree — so an app's records, files, and settings are ordinary contents
of your Lodekit folder, and backing up that folder preserves all of it. The
full layout is in [Your Lodekit folder](/guide/your-lodekit-folder/).

## The services

Apps don't reinvent the plumbing. Lodekit provides services every app can use:
a database for records, a small memory for quick notes, file storage, events,
scheduled tasks, safe doors to outside providers (like the weather forecast),
secrets, settings, and logs. Your agent builds with these, which
is why even a quick app gets durable storage and a settings page for free. Meet
them in the [Services overview](/services/overview/).
