Lodekit installs like any Mac app: one app bundle, dragged into place. Here's
the whole process, including the one extra step a first launch needs during
early access.

<Steps>

1. **Get `Lodekit.app`.**

   Lodekit is in early access: there's no public download yet, so you'll
   receive the app directly from the Lodekit team. It runs on Apple silicon
   Macs.

2. **Move it to Applications.**

   If it arrived as a zip, unzip it, then drag `Lodekit.app` into your
   `Applications` folder.

3. **First launch: right-click, then Open.**

   The early-access app is signed but not yet notarized, so macOS wants a
   nod from you the first time. Right-click (or Control-click) `Lodekit.app`
   and choose **Open**, then confirm in the dialog. macOS asks only once —
   after that, open it normally.

4. **Watch Lodekit set itself up.**

   On first run, three things happen:

   - The Lodekit icon appears in your menubar.
   - Your Lodekit folder is created at `~/Documents/Lodekit`, with the
     Greenhouse and Welcome starter apps already inside — plus a couple of
     ready-made gateway setups Greenhouse uses for its weather and sign-in
     demos.
   - The dashboard opens in your browser at `http://lodekit.localhost:7777`.

5. **Optional: turn on Launch at Login.**

   If you want Lodekit running whenever your Mac is on, enable **Launch at
   Login** — in the menubar menu, or in the dashboard under
   **Settings → General**.

</Steps>

:::tip
Open the Greenhouse app from the dashboard and poke around. It's a complete
garden-care app — adopt a plant, water it, watch the journal fill in. It shows
what your own apps will feel like.
:::

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing loads at `lodekit.localhost:7777`.** Click the Lodekit icon in the
menubar and check the status line. If it says stopped or error, choose
**Restart** from the same menu, then reload the page.

**macOS says the app "can't be opened".** That dialog appears when you
double-click instead of using the first-launch dance. Right-click
`Lodekit.app` and choose **Open** — the dialog gains an Open button, and macOS
remembers your choice from then on.

Lodekit is running. Next, give your agent its key:
[Connect your agent](/start/connect-your-agent/).
