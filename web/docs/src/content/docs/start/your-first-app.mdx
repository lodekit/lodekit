You've [installed Lodekit](/start/install/) and
[connected your agent](/start/connect-your-agent/). Time to build something.
This walkthrough uses a book log, but the shape works for any first app.

## Pick something tiny

Your first app should be small enough to describe in one breath: track the
books you're reading, log your workouts, keep a list of movies to watch. Small
apps arrive fast, and fast is what makes the loop click — describe, look,
adjust.

Don't worry about getting the idea perfect. Changing an app later is as cheap
as asking.

## What to say to your agent

Name the app, list what you want to record, and say how you want to see it:

```text
Build me a Lodekit app called book-log: I want to log books with title,
author, and the date I finished, and see them newest-first.
```

That's a complete brief. Your agent knows the rest — how a Lodekit app is put
together, where the data should live — from the skill you connected.

## What happens next

Your agent scaffolds the app through its Lodekit connection, then writes the
code. You can watch from the outside:

- New files appear in `apps/book-log/` inside your Lodekit folder.
- A **book-log** card appears on the dashboard, showing *building…*.
- A moment later the card flips to *ready*.

If the build hits an error, the card says so — and your agent can read the
error and fix it. You never touch the machinery.

## Open it

Click the card on the dashboard, or go straight to
`http://lodekit.localhost:7777/book-log/`. There's your app: a real web page,
running on your Mac, ready to log its first book.

## Make it yours

The first version is a starting point, not a verdict. Ask for changes the same
way you asked for the app:

- "Add a rating out of five to each book."
- "Let me mark a book as abandoned."
- "Show how many books I finished this year at the top."

Each request follows the same rhythm: your agent edits the files, the card
shows *building…*, and the change is live when it flips back to *ready*.

## Where your books went

Every book you log lands in `data/book-log/` in your Lodekit folder — sitting
right beside the app's source in `apps/book-log/`. Back up the folder and the
app and its records travel together. More in
[Your Lodekit folder](/guide/your-lodekit-folder/).

## What to try next

Two easy upgrades show off what apps get for free:

- **A settings knob.** Ask for one — "let me choose how many books show per
  page" — and it appears in the dashboard's Settings, no extra UI needed. See
  [Settings](/services/settings/).
- **A weekly summary.** Ask for "a task that adds a summary entry every Sunday
  of what I finished that week" and Lodekit runs it on schedule, even when the
  app isn't open in your browser. See [Tasks](/services/tasks/).

From here, the [dashboard guide](/guide/dashboard/) shows you around the
control room, and the [Services overview](/services/overview/) tours
everything your apps can lean on.
