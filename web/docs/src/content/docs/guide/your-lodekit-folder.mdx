Everything you'd miss lives in one folder: the Lodekit root at
`~/Documents/Lodekit`. Your apps' source code and every record they've saved
sit side by side, as plain files you can open in Finder.

<FileTree>
- Lodekit/
  - apps/ app source, written by your agent
    - greenhouse/
      - manifest.json
      - src/
      - …
    - book-log/
      - …
  - data/ each app's saved records
    - greenhouse/
      - db.sqlite
      - files/
    - book-log/
      - db.sqlite
    - _platform/ Lodekit's own records
  - gateways/ provider manifests
</FileTree>

## apps — the source

`apps/` holds one folder per app, named by its app id. Inside is the app's
source code — the files your agent writes and edits. There's nothing else:
apps carry no dependency folders (no `node_modules`, ever), so the tree stays
small, readable, and cheap to back up.

## data — the records

`data/` mirrors `apps/`: one folder per app id, holding everything that app
has saved. Each app gets its own database file (`db.sqlite`) and, when it
stores documents or images, a `files/` folder of real, ordinary files — the
photos in the Greenhouse app are JPEGs you can open straight from Finder.

The `_platform/` folder is Lodekit's own record-keeping — the event history,
your settings, and your secrets (encrypted). It belongs to the same backup.

:::caution
Browse `data/` freely, but don't move, rename, or edit its files while
Lodekit is running — apps are actively writing there, and edits behind their
back can corrupt records. To change data, work through the app (or ask your
agent).
:::

## gateways — the doors out

`gateways/` holds one folder per outside provider your apps can reach — each
with the provider's manifest and icon, written by your agent. It's part of
the same backup.

## Backups: one folder, whole story

Back up `~/Documents/Lodekit` and you're done. Time Machine, a synced drive,
a periodic copy — anything that covers that folder covers your apps, their
data, *and* their gateways. Restoring the folder onto a fresh Lodekit install
brings
everything back: the apps rebuild themselves from source, and the records are
already inside.

## What's deliberately elsewhere

Two things live outside the folder on purpose, because losing them costs you
nothing precious:

- **Cache** (`~/Library/Caches/Lodekit`) — the built, ready-to-serve version
  of each app. Delete it and Lodekit simply rebuilds; the cost is a short
  wait, never lost work.
- **State** (`~/Library/Application Support/Lodekit`) — operational history,
  mainly logs. Losing it means losing the diary of what happened, never your
  apps or data.

That split is why backing up one folder is genuinely the whole story.

The app that keeps all of this running sits in your menu bar — meet it in
[The menubar manager](/guide/manager/).
